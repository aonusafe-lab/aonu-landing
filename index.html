How it works

Three steps. No app install for your contacts.

Register

Log your meeting — a rental viewing, a road journey, a delivery pickup — with location, time, and counterparty details.

Monitor

A live timer runs. Your trusted contacts get a tracking link — no app install needed. They watch on any browser.

Respond

Timer expires or SOS is pressed — your trusted contacts are alerted automatically with your last known location.
